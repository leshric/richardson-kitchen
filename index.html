Day by day

Saturday, Nov 14

Heads-up
Everyone home. Rotisserie chicken, mac and cheese and potato salad are on the clock, so today eats them first.
Breakfast
Already behind you today. From tomorrow, the plan takes over.
Lunch: Lesh and Lesly
Tuna salad lettuce wraps: canned tuna with a spoon of Greek yogurt and mayo, on romaine, with baby carrots and a scoop of cottage cheese.
Lunch: boys
Finish yesterday’s mac and cheese with a griddled chicken patty each, a side of potato salad, and mandarins or grapes.
Dinner
Rotisserie chicken tacos from what’s on hand, since the shop is Sunday. Pull all the leg, thigh and quarter meat and warm it on the Blackstone with salsa. Romaine, Roma tomato, cheddar. Lesly’s Mexican rice (jasmine, tomato sauce, bouillon, the half onion, garlic powder).
Adult plate
Adults: low-carb. Taco salad bowl: chicken over romaine and tomato with salsa and a little cheddar. Skip the rice, or keep it to a couple of spoonfuls.
Boys’ plate
The unopened H-E-B taco shells plus a proper scoop of Mexican rice. Soft tortillas and avocado arrive with Sunday’s shop.
Mitchell and Daniel
Mitchell: pulled chicken is a whole cut, he’s fine. Daniel: chicken and melted cheese over rice if he won’t do a crunchy shell.
Snacks
Afternoon: Greek yogurt with frozen berries and a drizzle of honey. Evening: popcorn for the boys (Mitchell’s been eyeing it), cheese or cottage cheese for you two.
Tonight’s jobs
Move the chuck roast and the sirloin into the fridge to thaw (see the thaw schedule). Grocery run is Sunday morning.

Sunday, Nov 15

Heads-up
One H-E-B run first thing at Cross Creek Ranch, then steak night plus the one-hour Sunday prep that feeds the whole week.
Breakfast: Lesh
The usual: 2 eggs plus 240 g egg whites scrambled in a little butter, Ezekiel toast with peanut butter, a banana.
Breakfast: Lesly
Egg-white scramble with diced bell pepper, onion and a pinch of cheddar, one slice of Ezekiel toast, a handful of fresh blueberries.
Breakfast: boys
Kodiak Power Cakes on the Blackstone with the last of the peanut butter and frozen berries, scrambled eggs on the side. Hash brown casserole reheated for anyone still hungry.
Lunch: Lesh and Lesly
Taco salad bowls from last night’s chicken: romaine, tomato, salsa, avocado, a little cheddar, Greek yogurt as the sour cream.
Lunch: boys
Chicken quesadillas from the last of the rotisserie meat on fresh flour tortillas, potato salad, sliced Cosmic Crisp apples.
Dinner
Top sirloin on the Blackstone, rested and sliced across the grain. Normandy veg tossed in olive oil, salt and pepper, roasted on a sheet pan at 425°F until browned at the edges.
Adult plate
Adults: low-carb. A generous portion of steak and a big pile of veg. No starch needed.
Boys’ plate
Steak and veg plus the rest of the hash brown casserole and potato salad.
Mitchell and Daniel
Mitchell: steak is his thing. Daniel: cut his steak into bites; a chicken patty is the backup.
Snacks
Afternoon while you prep: hard-boiled eggs and pear slices. Evening: popcorn for the boys.
Tonight’s jobs
Run the Sunday prep checklist. Move about 2 lb of chicken breast into the fridge for Tuesday. Pack Lesh’s travel snack bag.

Adult plate
Adults: low-carb. Roast, carrots and broccoli, with a few bites of potato at most.
Boys’ plate

Mitchell and Daniel

House rules for every plate

One meal, two plates

Lesh and Lesly fill up on protein and veg with small, slower carbs (beans, brown rice, Ezekiel, low-carb tortillas). The boys get the same dinner plus the extra carb side.

Mitchell

Whole cuts only: steak, chicken breast, thighs, nuggets, patties. Nothing ground, no sausage, deli meat is a maybe. Peanut butter and jelly is always an acceptable plan B.

Daniel

After-school snack is small and protein-first, done by about 4:30. No full nugget plate after school. Every dinner names one thing he reliably eats.

Boys on school days

They eat school lunch. They can grab one protein bar on the way out, and mix their own shakes from the vanilla or chocolate powder.

Lesh’s breakfast

2 eggs plus 240 g egg whites in butter, Ezekiel toast with peanut butter, a banana, on every home morning.

Cook at home

Every meal on this page is cooked at home, apart from Lesh’s meals while away.
