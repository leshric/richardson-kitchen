Sweet potato casserole (Wednesday evening)

Filling: 4–5 lb sweet potatoes, baked or boiled until soft and mashed, plus 4 Tbsp melted butter, ⅓ cup brown sugar, ¼ cup milk, 2 beaten eggs, 1 tsp vanilla, ½ tsp cinnamon, and a pinch of salt. Spread in a buttered 9×13. Topping: ½ cup chopped pecans, ⅓ cup flour, ⅓ cup brown sugar, and 3 Tbsp cold butter, mixed to crumbs. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes until golden. Cool, cover, and refrigerate. Thursday: reheat at 350°F for 20–25 minutes. Roast the rest of the 8 lb bag for boy sides.
