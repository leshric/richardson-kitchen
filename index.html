Richardson Kitchen: Week of Nov 22–28, 2026

Last updated Sun Nov 22, 2026 (Central Time). Adults: low-carb, protein-forward. Boys: same meals plus carb sides. Mitchell: no ground or processed meat, and no deli. Kids are home this week, so lunches are at home. Shop: H-E-B Cross Creek Ranch, Sunday afternoon, one store.

Menu

  • Sun: Lunch: turkey and cheddar roll-ups (use the deli turkey today or Monday). Dinner: baked chicken and broccoli; rice or potatoes for the boys. After shopping: turkey straight into the fridge; Boston butt in the slow cooker overnight.
  • Mon (early dinner): Finish the deli turkey at lunch. Dinner by 5:30: pulled pork bowls with slaw; rice or potatoes for the boys. Freeze half the pork.
  • Tue: Lunch from leftover pork or baked chicken. Dinner: prime sirloin on the Blackstone with veg; potatoes or rice for the boys. Defrost the steak the same day in cold water.
  • Wed (leftover night): Leftover chicken and pork with veg. Evening: prep Thanksgiving sides and season the turkey.
  • Thu – Thanksgiving: Roast turkey, green beans, cauliflower mash, roasted sweet potatoes, stuffing and rolls, fresh cranberry relish. If the turkey is still icy in the morning, finish it in cold water.
  • Fri: Leftover turkey plates; turkey soup for adults; turkey sandwiches for the boys.
  • Sat: Filet night (all 8 filets). Same-day cold-water defrost. Salad for the adults, oven fries for the boys.

Breakfast: eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt with berries, cottage cheese, or protein pancakes. Kodiak tip: mix half Kodiak and half all-purpose flour, then add a spoon of sugar, vanilla, a pinch of salt, milk, an egg, and a little melted butter. Snacks: string cheese, yogurt, cottage cheese, fruit with peanut butter, hard-boiled eggs, protein bars.

Meal prep (Sunday evening)

  • Turkey into the fridge as soon as you get home
  • Slow-cook the Boston butt overnight (ready by 5:30 Monday)
  • Bake 3–4 lb chicken breasts at 425°F for lunches and Sunday dinner
  • Roast the broccoli heads with the chicken
  • Hard-boil a dozen eggs
  • Chop peppers and onion, cut fruit, portion cottage cheese, stock a snack bin
  • Wednesday night: stuffing base, cranberry relish, trim green beans, cube sweet potatoes, season the turkey

Defrost

  • Sun: 12-lb turkey into the fridge (about 3 days). If it’s still icy Thursday morning, finish in cold water; don’t microwave a whole turkey.
  • Everything else is same-day: Tuesday sirloin and Saturday filets in a sealed bag of cold water. Sunday’s chicken can be microwave-defrosted, then cooked right away.

Leftovers

Finish the deli turkey by Monday. Sunday’s baked chicken covers lunches and Wednesday night. Freeze half the pulled pork. Friday is leftover turkey (plates, soup, sandwiches); freeze the extra meat and simmer the bones for broth.
